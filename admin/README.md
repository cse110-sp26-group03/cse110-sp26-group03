# admin/

Quick reference for navigating this folder.

## Folder Structure

```
admin/
├── team.md                — Team roster and roles
├── branding/              — Logo and color assets
│   ├── details.txt        — Palette and font notes
│   ├── team_logo.jpg
│   └── logo/ray/three variants (black & white PNGs)
├── feedback/
│   └── feedback.md/.pdf   — Team feedback
├── meetings/
│   ├── meeting01–15       — Weekly team meeting minutes
│   ├── professor-powell-meeting.md
│   ├── pre-meeting-plans/ — Meeting agendas
│   ├── sprint-review/     — Sprint review writeups (review1, review2)
│   └── standups/          — Standup logs (sprint1–sprint5)
├── misc/
│   ├── rules.md           — Group contract
│   └── rules-*.pdf        — Signed individual agreements (per member)
├── photos/                — Team photos
└── videos/
    ├── teamintro.mp4
    └── Agile Team Status Video.mp4
```
